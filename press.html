Long (the App Store description)

A little mountain. A thousand possibilities.

Snowline is a calm, clever ski-resort strategy game. Draw a trail down the mountain with one finger, draw a lift back up, and watch tiny skiers bring your resort to life. They choose runs that suit their ability, wait in real lift lines, chase fresh snow and head home when they tire. When something goes wrong, the mountain shows you where, and one good change can fix the whole day.

The mountain is the interface

  • Draw trails downhill and lifts uphill, right on the terrain
  • A trail's difficulty comes from the ground it crosses: wind a switchback across a steep face and it becomes a gentler run
  • Every run is marked by shape as well as colour: green circle, blue square, black diamond
  • Undo and redo, pause any time, speed up when the resort hums

Skiers with minds of their own

  • Beginners, families and experts pick their own routes by ability, queue time and conditions
  • Lift lines grow from real demand and real lift capacity
  • Tap a lift or a run to see why it's busy and what would help

Lifts are the bottleneck

Trails carry skiers down; lifts carry them back up. Start with a surface tow, run chairlifts up the ridges and send a gondola to the high peaks. Upgrade a busy chair and watch where the crowd goes next.

Weather and snow you can read

  • The forecast warns you before a storm, a windy day or a warm spell
  • Fresh powder draws the experts; wind can close exposed lifts
  • Snow is finite: sunny low slopes thin out while high bowls hold their cover
  • Each morning, choose where the day's effort goes

Six ways to shape the day

Terrain parks, high-capacity lifts, warming huts, grooming stations, patrol outposts and snowmaking. Each one changes where skiers go, how long the snow lasts or how safely the mountain runs.

Three ways to play

  • Journey: five mountains, each with three medals to earn. Alpine Dawn teaches the basics, Pine Valley tests your lift lines, Glacier Basin your snow, High Ridge the wind, and Three Peaks everything at once
  • Zen: build at your own pace, with no way to fail
  • Daily Peak: a new mountain every day, the same for every player

Made to be comfortable

  • Difficulty never relies on colour alone
  • Reduced motion support, large touch targets and adjustable game speed
  • Plays fully offline, on iPhone in landscape and on iPad

One price, nothing else

No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No tracking. Your resorts are saved on your device.

Key features

  • The mountain is the interface. Draw trails downhill and lifts uphill with one finger, right on the terrain.
  • The terrain decides. A trail's difficulty comes from the ground it crosses; a switchback makes a gentler run. Every run is marked by shape and colour: green circle, blue square, black diamond.
  • Skiers with minds of their own. Beginners, families and experts choose their own routes. Lift lines grow from real demand and real capacity. Tap a lift or a run to see why it is busy.
  • Lifts are the bottleneck. Surface tows, chairlifts and gondolas. Upgrade a busy chair and watch where the crowd goes next.
  • Weather and snow you can read. Forecast warnings before powder, wind and warm days. Snow is finite, and each morning brings one operating choice.
  • Six features. Terrain park, high-capacity lift, warming hut, grooming station, patrol outpost and snowmaking.
  • Three ways to play. Journey across five mountains with three medals each, Zen with no way to fail, and Daily Peak, a new mountain every day.
  • Comfortable. Undo and redo, pause and game speed. Reduce Motion support and large touch targets. Plays fully offline.
  • One price, nothing else. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no tracking.
