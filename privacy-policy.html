Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version.

  • Snowline collects no data. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
  • No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking software.
  • Your saved resorts and settings stay on your device.
  • Game Center is optional. If you use it, Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy.

1. Who we are

Snowline is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

3. No network of its own

Snowline plays fully offline. It has no server, makes no network requests of its own and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software. The only online feature is Game Center, which is part of iOS and run by Apple.

4. Game Center

Game Center is optional. Every mode, including Daily Mountain, can be played without it. If you are signed in, Snowline reports your achievements and Daily Mountain scores to Game Center. Apple processes that information under its own privacy policy, and you control your Game Center profile, friends and visibility in Settings > Game Center.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in of our own.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your saves and settings stay on your device until you delete the game, which removes everything Snowline stored. Game Center data is managed by Apple in Settings > Game Center. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

7. Children

Snowline is a calm game suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Game Center is controlled by Apple and can be restricted by a parent with Screen Time. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Snowline keeps your data on your own device, so you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the game. We do not sell personal information. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

9. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Security

Snowline relies on the protections built into iOS and iPadOS: its saves sit inside the app's sandbox, protected by your passcode and the device's encryption.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the game.
