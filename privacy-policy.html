Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version.

  • Snowline collects no data. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".
  • No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • Your saved resorts, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The game makes no network requests of its own and plays fully offline.

1. Who we are

Snowline is a game for iPhone and iPad made by Jake Williams ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, where it goes and the choices you have. Questions: info@jakeawilliams.com.

3. No network of its own

Snowline has no server and no backend. It makes no network requests of its own and contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software. Every mode plays without a connection.

4. Permissions

Snowline asks for no permissions. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, health data or notifications. It does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

5. Apple services

If Snowline offers Game Center features and you are signed in to Game Center, the game may report things like scores or achievements to Game Center. Apple runs Game Center and processes that information under its own privacy policy; we receive nothing that identifies you. Game Center is never required to play, and you control your profile, friends and visibility in Settings > Game Center.

6. What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in of our own.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your saves and settings stay on your device until you delete the game, which removes everything Snowline stored. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

To delete it, touch and hold Snowline on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Copies inside your own device backups, in iCloud or on a computer, follow the rules of that backup.

8. Children

Snowline is a calm game suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Apple services such as Game Center are controlled by Apple and can be restricted by a parent with Screen Time. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Snowline keeps your data on your own device, so you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the game. We do not sell or share personal information. Nothing is tracked, so Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals need nothing further from us: the game and this site behave the same way with or without them. If you have a request we can help with, write to info@jakeawilliams.com.

10. This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or third-party resources; its fonts and images are served from the site itself. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

11. Security

Snowline relies on the protections built into iOS and iPadOS: its saves sit inside the app's sandbox, protected by your passcode and the device's encryption.

12. App Store privacy label

Snowline's privacy label on the App Store reads Data Not Collected, which matches this policy. If you ever find the two disagree, tell us and we will correct it.

13. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For a significant change we will also say so in the game.
