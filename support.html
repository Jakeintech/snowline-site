Email info@jakeawilliams.com. It helps to include your device, your iOS version, the mode and mountain you were playing, and what you expected to happen. A screenshot is welcome. A person reads every message.

Playing

How do I draw a trail?

Choose the Trails tool, then drag one finger downhill across the mountain. One finger only builds while a tool is selected. Two fingers always move the view: drag to pan, pinch to zoom.

How do I build a lift?

Choose the Lifts tool and drag from a low point to a higher one; lifts always run uphill. Start with a surface tow, run chairlifts up the ridges and send a gondola to the high peaks. When a chair gets busy, you can upgrade it.

How is a trail's difficulty decided?

By the terrain. A trail takes its difficulty from the ground it crosses, so the same descent drawn as switchbacks across a steep face becomes a gentler run. Difficulty is always shown by shape and colour together: green circle for beginner, blue square for intermediate and black diamond for advanced.

Why is a lift so busy, or my skiers unhappy?

Tap the lift or the run. It shows you the queue, what is slowing it down and what would help. Skiers want runs that suit their ability, a short wait and good snow, so a long line, a run that is too hard for the people on it, or a closure can send them home early.

What does the weather do?

The forecast warns you before it arrives. Fresh powder draws the experts, wind can close exposed lifts, and warm days thin the snow on low, sunny slopes. Each morning you choose where the day's effort goes.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes. Undo and redo are always on screen. You can also pause at any time and change the game speed.

What are the ways to play?

Journey takes you across five mountains, each a short scenario of a few in-game days with three medals to earn: Alpine Dawn, Pine Valley, Glacier Basin, High Ridge and Three Peaks. Zen lets you build at your own pace, with no way to fail. Daily Peak is a new mountain every day, the same for every player.

Saves, devices and settings

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Snowline has no account and no server. Deleting the game deletes its saves.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Snowline plays fully offline, in every mode.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. On iPhone the game plays in landscape. On iPad it works in any orientation.

What accessibility options are there?

Trail difficulty never relies on colour alone, so it reads without colour vision. Snowline follows Reduce Motion in Settings > Accessibility > Motion. Touch targets are at least 44 points, and you can pause or slow the game at any time.

Purchase and privacy

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Snowline is a premium game: you pay once on the App Store, and there are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the game: touch and hold Snowline on the Home Screen, tap Remove App, then Delete App. That removes your resorts, progress and settings. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete. The privacy policy has more.

What data does Snowline collect?

None. Your resorts are saved on your device, and the game makes no network requests of its own. The privacy policy has the detail.
