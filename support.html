Playing

How is a trail's difficulty decided?

By the terrain. A trail takes its difficulty from the real slope it crosses, so the same line drawn on gentler ground becomes an easier run. Difficulty is always shown by shape and colour together: green circle for beginner, blue square for intermediate, black diamond for advanced and double diamond for expert.

Why are my skiers frustrated?

Skiers arrive with different abilities and want runs that suit them, a short wait for a lift and a mountain that is open. Long queues, trails that are too hard or too easy for the people on the mountain, and weather closing things can all send skiers home unhappy. The summary at the end of a run tells you why.

What does the weather do?

The forecast strip shows what is coming. Snowfall changes the mountain, wind closes exposed lifts, and warm days soften the low runs. Planning around the forecast is part of the game.

Which lift should I build?

There are four: surface tow, chairlift, high-speed chair and gondola. Lifts are the uphill bottleneck, so watch the queues: a long line means that part of the mountain needs more capacity.

What are the modes?

Resort Run is a scored session of 12 to 18 minutes. Zen Mountain has no failure. Daily Mountain gives everyone the same mountain each day, with a Game Center leaderboard. Mountain Journey takes you across five mountains: Alpine Dawn, Pine Valley, High Ridge, Glacier Basin and Three Peaks. Endless Summit has no end to the run.

Game Center

Do I need Game Center?

No. Every mode plays without it. Game Center adds achievements and the Daily Mountain leaderboard if you are signed in.

My Daily Mountain score is not on the leaderboard.

Check that you are signed in under Settings > Game Center and that your device is online. Scores are sent by Apple's Game Center, which can take a little while to update.

Saves, devices and settings

Where is my progress saved?

On your device. Snowline has no account and no server. Deleting the game deletes its saves.

Does it work offline?

Yes, every mode plays without a connection. Only Game Center needs to be online, and it is optional.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. On iPhone the game plays in landscape.

What accessibility options are there?

Trail difficulty is shown by shape as well as colour, so it reads without colour vision. Snowline follows Reduce Motion in Settings > Accessibility > Motion. With VoiceOver on, you can hear a summary of how your resort is doing.

Purchase and privacy

Are there in-app purchases or ads?

No. Snowline is one price on the App Store. There are no ads and no in-app purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data does Snowline collect?

None. Your saves stay on your device, and Game Center is optional and handled by Apple. The privacy policy has the detail.
